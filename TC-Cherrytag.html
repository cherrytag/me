1. Accepting these terms

By submitting a subscription payment, or by using the Cherrytag Business Portal to create or manage a business listing, you agree to these Business Subscriber Terms & Conditions and to our Privacy Policy, on behalf of the business you represent. You confirm you're authorised to bind that business to this agreement.

2. The service

Cherrytag provides a simple, spend-fair loyalty rewards platform for small and medium cafés, restaurants, and businesses. Cherrytag works alongside your existing point-of-sale or payment system — there's no integration project required — and lets your customers earn and redeem rewards without downloading an app or carrying a card.

3. Subscription, trial and fees

  • Standard subscription: NZ$25 per month, billed monthly;
  • One month free trial for new Subscribers, no reward card or setup required to try it;
  • No setup fee and no lock-in contract — you can cancel at any time (see section 9);
  • Bespoke or configurable plans (custom point tiers, bonus rules or redemption caps) may be available on request and are agreed separately in writing.

4. Payment methods

You can pay by card via our payment processor, Stripe (your card details are handled directly by Stripe and are not stored by us), or by direct bank transfer. For bank transfers, you'll need to upload a receipt as proof of payment; we aim to verify bank transfers and confirm your subscription within 1 business day.

5. What's included

  • Promotional support such as a listing on the Cherrytag map, an in-app advertisement message, and general social media or hand-out material support, where offered to paid Subscribers.
