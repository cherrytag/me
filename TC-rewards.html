1. Accepting these terms

By creating a Cherrytag account, or by presenting a Cherrytag reward coupon or QR code at a participating business, you agree to these Rewards Terms & Conditions and to our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use Cherrytag.

2. Who can join

  • You must be 16 years or older and able to enter a binding agreement under New Zealand law;
  • One account per person, linked to a single email address;
  • Cherrytag is currently available for participating cafés, restaurants, businesses in your region.

3. Your account

No physical rewards card or app download is required. You sign up with your email address and choose a PIN, and a personal QR code is generated for your account — this is what you show a participating business to record a visit or redeem a reward.

  • Keep your PIN and account access private — you're responsible for activity on your account;
  • If you forget your PIN, you can request a reset link to your registered email;
  • Email or Tell us straight away if you think someone else has accessed your account. We will perform verification.

4. How the Cherrytag network works

Cherrytag is a shared loyalty network — instead of a separate card or app for every business, one Cherrytag account tracks your visits and points across every participating business. Points are recorded on a network-wide ledger, and coupons issued through Cherrytag can be used as described on the coupon itself and honoured by participating businesses in the network.

5. Earning points

Points are earned based on how much you spend in a single transaction at a participating business, at a guide rate of approximately 1.0 point ≈ NZ$1.00 of transaction value:

Some businesses may run their own configured tiers or bonus promotions within the network — where that's the case, the business will let you know at the point of sale.

6. Redeeming points and coupons

To keep rewards fair and meaningful for a business's genuine regulars, redemption isn't available immediately — it kicks in once you've built up a real relationship with the network:

  • You need a minimum of 4 repeat visits within the network before you're eligible to redeem; and
  • You need a qualifying points balance, generally 1.5 points or 2.5 points depending on the reward or business offer — the exact eligible amount is shown against each reward or coupon in the app.

Once eligible, you can generate a virtual reward coupon (shown as a QR code, typically worth 1.5, 2.5 or higher points depending on the reward chosen). Present it to staff to redeem — once scanned and confirmed, the coupon is used and the points are deducted from your balance. If you cancel a coupon before it's generated, the points are returned to your balance. Once the reward coupon is generated, it can't be cancelled.

Coupons and points can only be redeemed at participating Cherrytag businesses, are subject to that business having the advertised item or offer available, and may not be combined with other offers unless the business says otherwise.
